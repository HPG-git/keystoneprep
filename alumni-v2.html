Alumni Programs

Entrepreneur's Club

Keystone Prep High School proudly announces its Alumni Entrepreneur's Club. This club is only for alumni of Keystone Prep High School and Vincero Academy. The aim of this club is to engage the alumni of the school in entrepreneurial and investing opportunities and continued learning. It will feature expert guest speakers in the following fields: financial advice, investing, entrepreneurship, accounting and tax, banking, real estate, financial law, and social media exposure.

This club meets once a month on the 2nd Friday of the month, 7–8pm, at the school. Only alumni and their significant others may attend. Required attire for these events is "Business/Informal" or "Semi-Formal".
