Our Alumni

Our alumni are an essential part of the Keystone Prep story. The Alumni Program is designed to foster lifelong connections, celebrate professional achievements, and create meaningful opportunities for engagement beyond graduation.

Through this initiative, we are launching several structured pathways for involvement, including an Alumni Entrepreneurs Club, an Alumni Executive Committee, and the Office of Alumni Engagement. These programs will support mentorship, collaboration, networking, and strategic partnership opportunities that benefit both our graduates and the Keystone Prep community as a whole.

To stay informed about alumni events, leadership opportunities, and exclusive programming, we encourage all alumni to keep their contact information up to date. Accurate information ensures you receive timely invitations, announcements, and updates.

Update Your Information

One of our signature events will be the Annual Thanksgiving Alumni Social, scheduled to begin in 2026. This event will serve as a cornerstone gathering for reconnecting, building relationships, and strengthening our alumni network.

We look forward to growing this community together and welcoming our alumni back to Keystone Prep in meaningful and impactful ways.
